✿ How to play ✿

1
Pull back & let go. Like a slingshot: the cake flies the other way. Pull further for more power – or just tap where it should go.
2
Same cakes merge. Two identical cakes that touch become a bigger one. A pink heart in the preview means: match!
3
Keep the ring clear. Cakes drift to the middle while the pond slowly turns. A cake outside the ring for 4 seconds makes the pond overflow.
Goal: score high – bigger cakes and combos score more.
Hot streak: merge with every flick to multiply your points.
Next: tap it to swap your two cakes.
Magic Cake & Wand: earned on level-ups.
Fairy Bubbles: hit them for gifts.
