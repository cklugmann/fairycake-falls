✿ How to play ✿

1
Pull the cake back & let go. Like a slingshot: it flies the other way. The dotted preview shows its path through the water up to the first touch – what the hit sets off is yours to judge.
2
Same cakes merge. Two cakes of the same size that touch become a bigger one. A pink heart in the preview means: match!
3
Read the water. A current races round the edge and carries slow cakes with it; the calm middle draws them in and turns the pile. Fast flicks fly straight, slow ones curve – ride the current to reach the far side.
4
Keep the ring clear. A cake resting outside the ring for 5 seconds overflows the pond. Merge to make room!
Levels: every level is a recipe – bake the cake on the card. Stars come from your score at that moment. Clear level 5 of a world to open the next one.
Cake Book: every world bakes its own eleven cakes, and rare finds drift into the sling now and then. Merge a rare one to add it to the book.
Orders: each stage asks for a number of merges. Fill it and the pond whirls: the pile is shuffled, fresh cakes tumble in, your multiplier rises, a gift arrives.
Hot streak: merge with every flick to multiply your points.
Next: tap it to swap your two cakes (or press Space).
Magic Cake & Wand: gifts for finished orders. Fairy Bubbles hold more.
